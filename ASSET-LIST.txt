VUENA FREE MEGA STARTER - 68 CC0 GAME ASSETS (GLB, FBX)
=======================================================

(This is the GLB-only edition: the same 68 assets, GLB files only. The full download also has the FBX files and their texture folders.)

Hi, I am the person behind Vuena. I made this starter pack so you can try my work properly before spending a cent.
It collects the free samples of eleven of my packs into one download: 68 assets, ready to drop into Godot, Unity, Unreal, Blender or three.js.

TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - The packs were made at different times and in different styles (realistic PBR simulator props, blocky characters and creatures,
    low-poly toy cars, PS1-style horror props), so check the size column when you mix them. Scale each pack to suit your scene.
  - Simulator assets use PBR materials; the blocky packs use a small colour-palette texture; the PSX assets use tiny nearest-filter textures on purpose.
  - Files are unchanged from the individual free samples. I checked every GLB with the Khronos glTF-Validator: 0 errors.
  - Made with my own procedural Blender code (written with AI help). The meshes and textures are not AI-generated.
