WHAT IS INSIDE
--------------

    README.txt, LICENSE.txt

    FBX/       8 FBX files

  8 assets, each as a GLB and an FBX file = 8 GLB + 8 FBX files. No rigs, no animations.
  For the FBX files keep the folders as they are: they look for their textures in ../../Textures (relative paths, no personal paths inside).
  GLB files work on their own, with the textures embedded.

TECHNICAL NOTES
---------------
  - Realistic PBR props at real-world scale (metres), Y up.
  - The crate fills are single meshes made to drop into a wooden produce crate (the crate itself is in the full Store Simulator Kit). They also work
    on a plain table, in a box or in a basket. They look right at shopping distance; they are not food close-up quality.
  - I checked every GLB with the Khronos glTF-Validator: 0 errors. The FBX files were exported from Blender and I have not tested them in Unity or Unreal.
  - Made with my own procedural Blender code (written with AI help). The meshes and textures are not AI-generated.
